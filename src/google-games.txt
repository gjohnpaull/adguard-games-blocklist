! The "Games from Google" carousel (Baseball, Snake, Solitaire, Minesweeper,
! Block Breaker, PAC-MAN, Garden Gnomes, Color Tiles, Popcorn, Tic-Tac-Go,
! Champion Island) renders INLINE in the search results page. The network
! trace showed requests to only: google.com, gstatic.com, ogs.google.com,
! fonts.gstatic.com, lh3.googleusercontent.com. There is no game-specific
! host. Blocking any of those takes out Google Search, Gmail and Workspace.
!
! Google Doodle games live at the PATH google.com/logos/... - DNS has no
! visibility into paths.
!
! Chrome's offline dinosaur game is at chrome://dino and is entirely local.
! No network request is made at all. No network tool can block it.
!
! Doodle-game mirrors are hosted on sites.google.com/site/populardoodlegames
! and /site/more. sites.google.com cannot be blocked without breaking
! legitimate school and business Google Sites.
